Communication between Esp32 And RP PI  ------->

                  COMMUNICATION CHANNEL - 1 (URAT-1)

                   
                           Recipe 
      RP PI                                    ESP 32

1.  #TS-03*                                #TS-03ACK*                    (Total no of step)
2.  #RPM,1-100,2-150,3-300*                #RPM,1-100,2-150,3-300*       (RPM of each step)
3.  #DUR,1-00:01,00:15,00:55*              #DUR,1-00:01,00:15,00:55,ACK* (Test duration of each step)
4.  #SML,1-10,2-15,3-13*                   #SML,1-10,2-15,3-13,ACK*      (Sample amount of each step)
5.  #FL-2ml*                               #FL-2ml,ACK*                  (Flush sample amount)

                            TEST 
      RP PI                                    ESP 32

1.  #START-TEST*                           #START-TEST,ACK*              (start test)
2.  #PAUSE-TEST*                           #PAUSE-TEST,ACK*              (pause test)
3.  #STOP-TEST*                            #STOP-TEST,ACK*               (stop test)
4. FEEDBACK FROM ESP WHEN TEST END<--------#END-TEST,ACK*

                        HARDWARE CONTOL
      RP PI                                    ESP 32 
                         ARM MOTOR
1.  #START-RPM-150*                        #START-RPM-150,ACK*           (start test) 
2.  #STOP-RPM-150*                         #STOP-RPM-150*                (start test)
                        LIFTTING-ARM
3.  #LF-CU-UP*                             #LF-CU-UP,ACK*                (lifting column move up)       
4.  #LF-CU-DOWN*                           #LF-CU-DOWN,ACK*              (lifting column move down)
FEED BACK FROM ESP WHEN STIRREN REACH HOME<-(#LF-CL-HOME,ACK*) 
5.  #LF-CU-STOP*                           #LF-CU-STOP,ACK*              (lifting column stop)
